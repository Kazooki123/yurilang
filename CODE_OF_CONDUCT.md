# YuriLang Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in YuriLang a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of **age, gender, sexuality, religion, race, experience level, or background.**

YuriLang is a creative and expressive project, and we aim to keep it welcoming, respectful, and safe for all.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- 🧡 Being respectful and inclusive
- 🤍 Giving constructive feedback
- 🩷 Supporting new contributors
- 💜 Respecting different perspectives and experiences

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- ❌ Harassment or discrimination of any kind
- ❌ Personal attacks or insults
- ❌ Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior
- ❌ Any form of hate speech

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

**They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, or contributions that violate these rules.**

---

## Scope

This **Code of Conduct applies** within all YuriLang project spaces, including:

- GitHub repository
- Issues and pull requests
- Discussions and community spaces

---
